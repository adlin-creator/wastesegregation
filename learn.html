🌿 What is Waste?

Waste is any material that is no longer useful and is discarded by people. Improper disposal of waste can cause pollution and harm both humans and nature.

♻️ Types of Waste

  • Biodegradable Waste – Food scraps, leaves, paper, and other materials that decompose naturally.
  • Non-Biodegradable Waste – Plastic, glass, metals, and materials that take a very long time to break down.
  • E-Waste – Old computers, mobile phones, batteries, and electronic devices.
  • Hazardous Waste – Chemicals, paints, medical waste, and toxic substances.

🏥 How Waste Affects Human Health

  • Causes respiratory problems due to polluted air.
  • Spreads diseases through contaminated water and surroundings.
  • Attracts mosquitoes, flies, and harmful insects.
  • Can cause skin infections and allergies.
  • Pollutes food and drinking water sources.

🌍 How Can We Reduce Waste?

  • Reduce the use of plastic products.
  • Reuse items whenever possible.
  • Recycle paper, glass, plastic, and metal.
  • Segregate waste into wet and dry categories.
  • Use reusable bags and water bottles.
  • Compost biodegradable waste.
  • Dispose of e-waste responsibly.

🌱 Good Practices We Should Follow

  • Keep public places clean.
  • Do not litter roads and streets.
  • Use dustbins for waste disposal.
  • Participate in community clean-up activities.
  • Plant more trees and protect green spaces.
  • Create awareness about waste management.
